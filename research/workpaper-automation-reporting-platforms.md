# Reporting platforms: operational evidence

Research date: 2026-11-06. Primary sources only. Help documentation is stronger operational evidence than product pages; release announcements establish what was available at their publication date. No hands-on product validation performed.

## Assessment

Workiva and Fieldguide address substantial portions of recurring financial reporting, but principally through a managed, connected data/reporting environment. Neither reviewed documentation establishes that an arbitrary folder of historical Excel workpapers can be rolled forward, refreshed, reconciled, and turned into complete compliant financial statements without initial mapping and review. Fieldguide's newer PDF-to-draft capability materially narrows that gap, but creates a native platform document. This is an inference from the workflows below, not a vendor limitation claim.

## Workiva

- **2026 AI overlap:** September 10 help explicitly lists an AI Roll forward workflow moving data and project artifacts to the next period, alongside benchmarking, tie-outs, and sustainability disclosure workflows. Access depends on workspace entitlement. This is operational primary evidence and means Workiva should not be characterized as purely manual rollover. The July 29 launch announces Tie-Out, Benchmarking and Sustainability Disclosure agents for advanced tiers, but does not establish a July release date for Roll Forward and Prep Agent. [Global companion help](https://support.workiva.com/hc/en-us/articles/51755818511124-Workiva-AI-is-now-your-global-companion), [July AI announcement](https://newsroom.workiva.com/press-releases/workiva-launches-specialized-ai-agents-and-intelligence-layer-advance-high-stakes).
- **AI review limits:** Tie-out Assistant documentation describes single-document numerical consistency, reviewer verification/overrides, and notes. Rerunning clears notes and resolved items, with an option to retain tickmarks. Its early-access notice specifies a September 14, 2026 evaluation end subject to change; that expired date does not establish November GA or unavailability. Confirm current access. [Use Tie-out Assistant](https://support.workiva.com/hc/en-us/articles/50700507481364-Use-Tie-out-Assistant).
- **Existing Excel:** XLSX import includes formulas, formatting, and data; import may create a workbook, add sheets, or replace values in identically named sheets. Documented limits include 16 MB per XLSX and 32,767 characters per cell. Embedded files must be removed and reattached after import. This supports migration and refresh, but does not establish perfect preservation of macros, external workbook dependencies, Power Query, or arbitrary Excel features. [Create or import a spreadsheet](https://support.workiva.com/hc/en-us/articles/360035643172-Create-or-import-a-spreadsheet), updated May 18, 2026.
- **Period rollover:** copy files/folders/processes, choosing how links are handled. Workiva recommends copying all source files for typical roll-forwards, so copied destinations point to copied sources. Permissions persist; revision history resets. This is explicit dependency-aware rollover, after links exist. [Copy a file or folder](https://support.workiva.com/hc/en-us/articles/360035639992-Copy-a-file-or-folder).
- **Data refresh:** connect a sheet to a Wdata query, then refresh the incoming connection from the toolbar or Connections panel. This documents a configured source connection rather than autonomous recognition of new client files. [Connect sheets to source Wdata queries](https://support.workiva.com/hc/en-us/articles/360035643272-Connect-sheets-to-source-Wdata-queries).
- **Adjustments:** Workiva recommends spreadsheet adjustment columns for rounding, cell-history auditability, and easier removal on rollover. Do not confuse this with evidence that Workiva independently proposes or posts all accounting adjustments. [Format values and numbers](https://support.workiva.com/hc/en-us/articles/360035643072-Format-values-and-numbers), updated October 1, 2026.
- **Statement propagation:** vendor training illustrates an extended trial balance rolled into presentation sheets and source-linked into statement documents. Updating the sources updates linked destinations. The key prerequisite is building those mappings and links. [Global statutory reporting demonstration material](https://support.workiva.com/hc/en-us/article_attachments/4408977184788).
- **Review:** Workiva documentation describes sending specific document revisions for read-only review and comments. This is review orchestration, not proof that accounting disclosure completeness is automatically established. [Discover Workiva Documents](https://support.workiva.com/hc/pt-br/article_attachments/360077870711).
- **Regional applicability:** official global statutory reporting FAQ describes onboarding from customers' existing financial statements, commonly Word documents already refined with auditors and accepted by local regulators. It describes relying on advisory partners for ongoing regulatory updates and final human review of translations before submission. This is particularly relevant to local-GAAP/IFRS adoption: do not assume a universal automatically maintained statutory template. This is undated training material and should be treated as architectural context, not a current contractual commitment. [Global Statutory Reporting FAQ](https://support.workiva.com/hc/es-es/article_attachments/360083986691).

## Fieldguide

- **Managed setup:** the July 2026 getting-started guide requires a firm financial-audit framework/chart of accounts. It recommends engagement, lead-sheet, statement, report, and form templates, with forms supplying client details and footnotes. Client accounts map to the firm framework. The documented traditional output workflow uses report templates and mail-merge tags. [Financial audit: Getting started](https://help.fieldguide.io/en/articles/10330326-financial-audit-getting-started).
- **Refreshed TB:** replacement updates matching accounts, adds new accounts, and retains absent accounts at zero. Existing account mappings are kept; new mappings must be supplied and reviewed. The July 2025 instructions require populating a downloadable Excel import template. [Replace a trial balance](https://help.fieldguide.io/en/articles/9893814-replace-a-trial-balance).
- **Newer ingestion claim:** the current product page advertises AI reading TB files as delivered without templates, while also describing standardized uploads. It says hierarchy, mappings, and prior-year balances carry forward. This is newer marketing evidence that may supersede parts of the older help workflow; ask for a demonstration on real messy client files before assuming template-free ingestion universally. [Trial Balance and Consolidation](https://www.fieldguide.com/products/audit/trial-balance-and-consolidation).
- **Mapping to statements:** client accounts map to firm accounts, which link to statement line items within sections. Totals recalculate when linked accounts change. A firm account can link to only one line item at a time; users must unlink it before assigning it elsewhere. [Configure a financial statement](https://help.fieldguide.io/en/articles/10355059-configure-a-financial-statement).
- **Adjustments:** the vendor changelog documents selecting the target TB in the adjustment pane and posting there; the adjustment is then viewed within that particular TB. This is concrete native adjustment support. [Post Adjustments to Any Trial Balance](https://changelog.fieldguide.io/post-adjustments-to-any-trial-balance-330224).
- **Rollover controls:** admins choose per-column retain/clear/lock behavior; documents may all roll forward, only folders may roll forward, or selected documents may be used. Forms retain data with dates advanced a year. Request statuses reset; milestones lose dates and owners. Sample populations become empty placeholders, with a documented 5,000-row placeholder import limit. Thus rollover requires explicit rules and fresh evidence. [Engagement rollover](https://help.fieldguide.io/en/articles/8370742-engagement-rollover).
- **Excel compatibility:** the add-in connects a workbook to an engagement/framework and exposes custom functions, tickmarks, and static TB export. Formula-driven financial data updates are explicitly one-way from Fieldguide to Excel. Existing Excel workpapers can therefore participate, but must be connected with these functions; documentation does not promise automatic reconstruction of their business logic. [Excel add-in](https://help.fieldguide.io/en/articles/11570420-excel-add-in-for-financial-audit), [Excel functions](https://help.fieldguide.io/en/articles/11571224-excel-functions-for-financial-audit).
- **Important 2026 advancement:** the August 17 announcement says Drafting Agent is live: it reads the prior-year issued PDF, preserves structure and firm formatting, advances dates, and populates a native cloud document from current engagement data. Cells identify TB links, derived formulas, and manual overrides; preparers review the draft. The same announcement describes Tie-Out and Disclosure Agents as forthcoming. [Field Financials drafting launch](https://www.fieldguide.com/blog/field-financials-drafting-agent).
- **Availability ambiguity as of research date:** current product pages describe footnote drafting from standards, firm libraries, and live data, plus math/internal-consistency review. These are direct competitive claims, not features to omit. However, no dated operational help/release evidence found in this research resolves exactly when every agent became generally available after the August roadmap. Confirm entitlement, release status, and supported accounting standards during evaluation. [Field Financials](https://www.fieldguide.com/products/agents/field-financials), [Financial Statements](https://www.fieldguide.com/products/audit/financial-statements).
- **Dated launch confirmation:** August 12 explicitly says Field Financials is available to all customers, with Drafting Agent first and Review & Tie-Out and Disclosure agents planned for the fall. This reinforces separating the live drafting launch from the subsequent agent availability question. [Launch announcement](https://www.fieldguide.com/blog/fieldguide-launch-and-brand-refresh).
- **Regional applicability:** the reviewed operational sources do not enumerate supported statutory jurisdictions, local-GAAP disclosure libraries, or local filing formats. Generic references to applicable standards do not establish coverage for a particular country or entity class. This is an evidence gap, not proof of absence.

## DataSnipper: review versus preparation

Financial Statement Suite starts with existing statements and performs/document checks. Its FAQ explicitly says it cannot test TB-to-statement consistency within FSS; Document Matching is the suggested separate tool. Numeric checks exclude numbers embedded in paragraph text, and some intended sums need manual checking. These are clear limits on positioning FSS as end-to-end preparation software. [FSS FAQ](https://knowledge.datasnipper.com/en/articles/694183-faqs-for-financial-statement-suite).

It exports marked-up statements and findings; tickmarks can carry into revised statement versions. This is useful review-cycle automation, distinct from generating the underlying statements. [Export FSS documents](https://knowledge.datasnipper.com/en/articles/741981-how-to-export-documents-from-financial-statement-suite).

The 2026 release notes describe Disclosure Agents as preview in the US and UK only, including a regional checklist library. Do not generalize that preview to all jurisdictions or equate checklist review with complete financial-statement preparation. [2026 release notes](https://knowledge.datasnipper.com/en/articles/702973-2026).

## Evaluation questions grounded in these gaps

1. Demonstrate a full cycle using the buyer's unmodified Excel workbook, prior-year issued report, new raw TB and supporting schedules, then a late corrected TB.
2. Identify what requires first-year mapping, formulas, templates, connectors, or implementation services; time that setup separately from later-year savings.
3. Show new/deleted accounts, manual adjustments, comparative restatements, missing evidence, manual overrides, and downstream changes in notes.
4. Show the supported accounting framework and jurisdiction, who maintains disclosure content, and which checks remain preparer responsibilities.
5. For Fieldguide, demonstrate production availability of drafting, tie-out, and disclosure agents separately; for Workiva, demonstrate preservation of the buyer's actual Excel-specific features rather than generic XLSX import.
