# Glossary Map

## Contexts

- [Audit Workbench](./idea1-audit-workbench/GLOSSARY.md): helps auditors plan and perform line-item-specific procedures faster
- [Filing Analyzer](./idea2-filing-analyzer/GLOSSARY.md): explains ratios and revised values in real SEC filings

## Relationships

- **Audit Workbench ↔ Filing Analyzer**: both judge whether reported numbers are misstated; the Workbench works on a client's books before issuance, the Analyzer on public filings after issuance
