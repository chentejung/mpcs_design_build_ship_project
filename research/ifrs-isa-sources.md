## Implications for the app
- No verbatim IFRS or ISA text beyond short, attributed references. Cite paragraph numbers (e.g. "IFRS 15.31", "IFRS 9.5.5.15", "ISA 505.7") and paraphrase in your own words. The IFRS T&C give no general quotation allowance, and an app counts as "product development", which needs consent. UK/EU quotation exceptions for criticism or review may cover very short quotes, but that is a legal judgment and not verified here.
- Build your own worked examples, e.g. your own provision matrix and IFRS 15 five-step scenarios. Do not copy the IFRS IEs or the Big 4 illustrative FS.
- Link out to ifrs.org, iaasb.org and the Big 4 PDFs rather than embedding them.
- For anything beyond paraphrase, email customerservices@ifrs.org or permissions@ifac.org.
