# Accountant versus auditor: open product decisions

2026-11-06. The user has reopened the initial customer choice: company accounting teams versus auditors examining those companies. No pivot has been approved. The previous all-auditor ambition remains context, not a constraint on this exploration.

## Terms for this discussion

- Company accounting team: prepares, reconciles and maintains the company's financial records and reporting.
- External audit team: examines a client's financial information and documents its audit work.
- Internal audit is a separate potential segment; working inside a company does not make an internal auditor a financial-statement preparer.
- Reconciliation establishes agreement or explains differences between records; agreement alone does not establish that underlying transactions are valid or that a population is complete.

These are discussion distinctions, not a new settled domain model.

## Current plan's overlap

Account mapping, AR rollforward, comparison to schedules and Excel export could assist either audience. Procedure/assertion links, journal-entry selection and audit review documentation currently favor auditors. Company use would require discovery around recurring close, ownership of corrections, sign-offs, periods and integration with accounting systems. Shared arithmetic does not imply shared buying decisions or workflows.

## Decision tree

1. Establish independent inputs: near-term objective/deadline; access to practitioners/reviewers/buyers; personally observed costly task; available permitted data; initial jurisdiction; willingness to focus the first workflow.
2. Use those answers plus market evidence to choose the first owner of the problem: company preparer or external auditor. If both remain plausible, run comparable interviews before choosing.
3. Then choose a single recurring task, its input/output boundary, buyer, and success measure.
4. Then settle import requirements, review trail, processing location, AI role, workflow integration and exclusions.
5. Finally define a comparable pilot and the evidence required to expand.

Do not ask for feature prioritization or detailed architecture before the task and user are settled.

## Working recommendation, not a decision

If access and pain evidence are equal, test the auditor workflow first because the repo's existing plan and the user's stated former-auditor experience align with it. This is a project-fit inference, not evidence of a larger or less competitive market. Prefer company accounting if there is materially stronger access to repeat use, a specific unresolved task and permitted examples. Do not build both interfaces at once simply because both use reconciliations.
